1. Introduction

ASD 123 Inc. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the ASD-123 App and Website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App or Website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services.

2.1 Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name (parent/guardian or clinician name)
  • Email address
  • Account credentials (username and hashed password)
  • Account type (parent/guardian or clinician)
  • Subscription status and payment information (processed through third-party payment processors)

2.2 Student Data

When you create a student profile, we collect:

  • Student first name (no last name required)
  • Age or date of birth (month and year only)
  • Exercise preferences and settings
  • Progress data and performance metrics
  • Transcribed text from voice exercises (see Section 3 below)

We minimize the collection of student personal information and do not require full names or precise dates of birth.

2.3 Usage and Technical Data

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the App or Website:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent, interaction patterns)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, pages viewed)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

2.4 Cookies and Website Data

Our Website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve user experience:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the Website
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

3. Voice Data Processing

3.1 No Audio Storage

We do not store voice recordings. This is a core privacy principle of our App.

3.2 How Voice Processing Works

When a student speaks during an exercise:

  1. The audio is temporarily captured and processed in real-time or briefly cached
  2. The audio is transcribed to text using voice recognition technology
  3. The audio recording is immediately deleted after transcription
  4. Only the transcribed text is retained for educational purposes

3.3 Transcribed Text Retention

We retain transcribed text to:

  • Track student progress and performance over time
  • Provide feedback to students and clinicians
  • Generate reports and analytics
  • Improve the accuracy of voice recognition

Transcribed text is associated with student profiles and protected according to this Privacy Policy.

12.2 What Clinicians Can Access

12.3 Revoking Clinician Access

Parents and guardians can revoke clinician access at any time through the App settings. Upon revocation, the clinician will immediately lose access to the student's data.
